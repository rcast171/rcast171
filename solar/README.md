Slide archives are text extracts, not full visual reproductions.

These materials demonstrate the scope of research and training exposure. They do not independently verify authorship of every section, team-training delivery, measured sales results, or built infrastructure. Some source articles identify Roberto Castillo; do not extend that attribution automatically to every document. AI-assisted content and historical claims require separate factual review before reuse as current guidance.

Incentives, tax credits, rates, pricing, utility rules, warranty and company-ranking claims are preserved as historical wording, including conflicting versions. The archive is not current financial or energy advice. Airport concepts in the register are concepts, not completed developments. Employer and third-party names remain attributable to their sources; this repository grants no blanket license to those materials.
